COMPANY: GoAified Demo Organization
DOCUMENT: Employee Handbook
VERSION: 1.0

1. Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break during the workday.

Different schedules may apply to certain roles with manager approval.

2. Attendance

Employees must arrive on time and follow their assigned working schedules.

Daily attendance must be recorded through the designated attendance system.

Employees who expect to arrive late or miss work must inform their immediate manager.

3. Onboarding

New employees must complete the organization's onboarding process.

The onboarding process includes submitting employment documents, receiving company equipment, creating employee accounts, and attending orientation sessions.

Managers are responsible for explaining initial job responsibilities.

4. Workplace Conduct

Employees must communicate respectfully and maintain professional behavior.

Employees must cooperate with colleagues and complete assigned responsibilities.

Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

5. HR Contact Procedures

Employees can contact the HR department for questions regarding employment records, attendance, leave balances, onboarding, and workplace policies.

HR requests must be submitted through the designated HR system or official communication channel.

6. Policy Updates

Employees are responsible for reading and following company policies.

The organization may update its handbook when necessary.

Employees will be informed about important policy changes through official communication channels.
