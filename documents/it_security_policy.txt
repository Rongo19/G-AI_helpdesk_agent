COMPANY: GoAified Demo Organization
DOCUMENT: IT Security Policy
VERSION: 1.0

1. Password Requirements

Employees must use strong and unique passwords for company accounts.

Passwords must contain at least 12 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Employees must not share passwords with other individuals.

Employees should enable multi-factor authentication wherever available.

2. Device Security

Employees must protect company-issued laptops, phones, and other devices.

Devices must be locked whenever they are unattended.

Employees must install security updates regularly.

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT support team immediately.

Employees must not install unauthorized software on company devices.

3. Data Protection

Company information must only be stored and shared through approved systems.

Employees must not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals.

Sensitive information must be protected when accessing company systems outside the office.

4. Phishing Prevention

Employees must be careful when opening unexpected emails, links, and attachments.

Employees must not share passwords or confidential information through suspicious websites or messages.

Suspicious emails must be reported to the IT support team.

Employees should verify unusual requests before responding.

5. Security Incident Reporting

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents.

Examples include phishing attacks, malware infections, unauthorized account access, and accidental data disclosure.

Reports must be submitted through the designated IT helpdesk channel.

Employees should provide relevant details about the incident.

6. Employee Responsibilities

All employees are responsible for following IT security guidelines.

Employees must participate in security awareness training when required.

Questions about security policies must be directed to the IT support team.
