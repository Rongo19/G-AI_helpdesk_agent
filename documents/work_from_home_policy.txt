COMPANY: GoAified Demo Organization
DOCUMENT: Work From Home Policy
VERSION: 1.0

1. Eligibility

Full-time employees whose roles can be performed remotely are eligible to request work-from-home arrangements.

Eligibility depends on job responsibilities, performance, and team requirements.

2. Approval Process

Employees must submit work-from-home requests through the designated HR system.

All requests require approval from the immediate manager.

Approval may depend on business requirements and team availability.

3. Remote Working Expectations

Employees must remain available during their agreed working hours.

Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings and complete assigned tasks on time.

Employees must regularly communicate their work progress to their managers.

4. Workspace and Equipment

Employees must maintain a safe and suitable remote workspace.

Company devices must be used according to the IT Security Policy.

Employees are responsible for protecting company information while working remotely.

5. Changes and Exceptions

Changes to approved work-from-home schedules must be discussed with the manager.

Exceptions require approval from the department head or HR department.

6. HR Support

Employees can contact the HR department for questions about work-from-home eligibility and approval procedures.
