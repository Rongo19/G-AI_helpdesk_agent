COMPANY: GoAified Demo Organization
DOCUMENT: Employee Leave Policy
VERSION: 1.0

1. Annual Leave

Every full-time employee is entitled to 20
annual leave days per calendar year.

2. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 8 casual leave
days per calendar year.

3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leave
days per calendar year.

4. Leave Approval

Employees must submit leave requests
through the designated HR system.

Leave approval depends on manager
authorization and organizational requirements.

5. Leave Balance

Employees can contact the HR department
to verify their remaining leave balance.
